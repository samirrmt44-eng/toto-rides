🔐 Driver Approval System Kaise Kaam Karega?

  1. Driver driver.html par register karega - Photo, Name, Phone, Vehicle No.
  2. Registration turant aapke paas pending list me ayega (Firebase: toto_pending_approvals)
  3. Aap admin.html ya driver.html > Approvers me approve karoge.
  4. Aap kisi bhi driver ko Approver bana sakte ho - wo bhi approve kar sakega.
  5. Approve hote hi driver ko notification - Ab wo Online ja sakta hai.
  6. Customer ko koi approval nahi - Sab direct join.
