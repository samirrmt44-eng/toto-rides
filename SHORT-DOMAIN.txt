Short domain guide:
SHORT DOMAIN KAISE BANAYE:

1. 4EVERLAND me:
   - Project > Domains tab > Add Custom Domain
   - Free me aapko milega: toto-xxxx.4everland.app (already short)
   - Custom domain kharido: toto-jharkhand.com ya totoride.in (Rs 300/year Hostinger)
   - DNS me CNAME add karo jo 4everland batayega

2. Fleek me:
   - Fleek automatically deta hai: your-site.on.fleek.co (chota)
   - Custom: fleek.co > Domains > Add Domain > toto-rides.in

3. ENS Domain (Web3 permanent):
   - app.ens.domains par jao
   - toto-jharkhand.eth kharido (~$10/year)
   - IPFS hash se link karo - lifetime Web3 domain

4. URL Shortener (Instant):
   - tinyurl.com ya is.gd par jao
   - Apni lambi IPFS link paste karo
   - Short link banao: tinyurl.com/toto-ramgarh

RECOMMENDED SHORT:
- toto-rides.4everland.app (rename project to toto-rides in 4everland settings)
- toto.4ever.app (if available)
- totorides.in (buy)
